Splitfool

Instructions

  1. For each person that got food, enter their name
    (eg. To enter two people, you type 'John', press 'enter', and then type 'Jane'. The input stays focused.)
  2. For each person, for every item they got, click 'Add item', and enter the cost of this dish. You can also enter the dish name if you like
  3. Once you've added all the dishes on the bill to someone's tab, enter the entire amount paid, including tax and tip.
    Splitfool will show how much each person owes, in proportion to how much their food cost.
  4. If more than one person put money down (let's say you all chipped in a couple bills from your wallets), you can go back to the 'fools' section, and and type in the 'Paid:' input. Splitfool will show everyone's net settlement.

    If you have multiple people who put down money, it's often easier to designate one person to take all they money and then distribute it out as needed, because it's simpler than everyone sending out 5 venmo payments. Everyone pays one person, and that one person pays people out.
